## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

73.8% of Nashville's unpermitted-STR complaints are about parcels with no permit record at all.

Metro's property standards layer holds 568 cases recorded with the exact problem string "host - no strp". Fused on parcel to the STR permits file, 419 of those 568 sit on parcels that appear nowhere in the permits file at any status. 38 (6.7%) sit on parcels with a currently issued permit.

The wider picture: 1,491 short-term rental cases in 43,283 property standards cases received since October 2023. STR cases arrive by email 98.1% of the time, against 4.3% of cases overall. One limit rides with the fusion: permit status is as pulled on Nov 1, 2026, not as of the day each case was received, and the brief prints that limit with the figures.
